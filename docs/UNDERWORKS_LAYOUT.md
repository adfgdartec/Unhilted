# The Underworks: room plan

Generated by `tools/level/build_underworks.py` (re-run it after editing; it
overwrites `scenes/rooms/underworks/`). Same movement rules as the Heap
(`HEAP_LAYOUT.md`): 40 px steps, running jumps up to about 130 px, slide or
dash jumps to about 170 px. A standing robot is 28 px tall, so never leave
28 px or less of headroom over a floor someone has to walk.
Verified by `tools/underworks_test.tscn` (a bot plays each route).

## Swing numbers (Shard II)
- The wire is 150 px. The robot keeps falling while the blade flies, so a ring
  at more than about 110 px from where you throw usually recalls before it
  catches. Rings are placed 90 to 110 px from the top of a running jump.
- Ring height over a gap: about 150 px above the floor you jump from, 120 to
  130 px horizontally past the edge. That carries a 240 px pit with a pillar
  every ~280 px.
- A ring next to a ledge you can't reach: the ledge's near edge should be
  60 to 120 px past the ring and 30 to 60 px above the ring's lowest swing
  point. A walkway directly over the swing blocks the release (you hit its
  underside), so keep the walkway's near edge past the release point.

## Quest items
- Relief valves (Pressure Drop): Bellows Row roofs, Boiler Works catwalk, Condensate Falls floor.
- Letters (Letters Home): Pressure Gauge Hall ledge, the tram roof in the Old Tram Tunnel, the Cistern's far ledge.
- The Lamplighter's lantern (The Last Lamplighter): dropped by the mini-boss.
All three count even if found before the quest is taken (they're key items).
