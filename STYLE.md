# Unhilted: visual style guide

## Direction: painterly HD pixel, at dusk
Big shapes, few values, strong colour blocks, specific landmarks. The Heap is lit by a low smog sun from the upper right: warm rims on every top edge, cool slate shadows. Cyan belongs only to the robot, its blade and docks, so it is the one cool accent in every scene. Avoid the generic neon-night-rain look; avoid procedural randomness that shows (random windows, sine hills, tile grids).

All non-character art is generated by `tools/art/build_backdrops.py` (hand-composed backdrops), `tools/art/build_art.py` (tilesets) and `tools/art/build_props.py` (props, interactables, pickups, effects, UI) from ONE palette in `tools/art/pix.py`. Edit the script, rerun it, and Godot re-imports the PNGs. Characters (robot, NPCs, enemies, boss) are still placeholder shapes drawn in code, by design for now.

## Resolution and scale
- Base resolution 640 x 360, 16 x 16 tiles, nearest-neighbour filtering, 2D transforms snapped to pixels.
- Art is drawn at native size and never scaled down from larger images.

## Palette (single shared palette, keys in `pix.py`)
| Group | Use |
|---|---|
| Scrap neutrals `k d m g l w` | Ground, metal, props. `k` is the outline colour. |
| Rust `r R o O` | Decay on modern junk. |
| Ancient brass `b B y Y` | Only for the old world: the robot's pod, buried gear hints. Brass means "older than the city". |
| Neon `c C p P a A` | Only on things that are powered. Cyan = the robot's blade/tech and docks; amber = warm human light and warnings; pink = city signage. |
| Toxic `t T`, cloth/wood `q Q e E`, glass `s S`, warning `x X`, fire `f F` | Specific materials. |
| Backdrop silhouettes `1-9` | Backgrounds only; low contrast, no outlines. |

## Rendering rules
- Props and items: 1 px darkest (`k`) outline; light from the upper left (top rows lighter, undersides darker).
- Backdrops: no outlines, few values, horizontal wrap at 640 px, parallax by distance (sky 0, skyline 0.06, far ridge 0.2, near hills 0.42).
- Ground fill must stay quieter than props and characters: dark base, few low-contrast details. Walkable top edges carry the brightest row so platforms read at a glance.
- Large background props are dimmed (`Prop.background = true`) so they never read as solid or interactive.
- Light: banded additive glow (`art/fx/glow.png`), only on real light sources (neon, lamps, fire, docks, warning lamps). No blur.
- Gameplay signals use reserved colours: orange-marked scrap walls = Shard I can cut; amber ring = grapple point; red = enemy telegraph.

## Animation timing
- Props: 2 to 4 frames. Neon flicker ~1.3 fps, fire 8 fps, dock charge lines 8 fps, CRT static 6 fps.
- Story-state frames: `Prop.lit_when_quest` switches frame 0 to 1 when a quest is turned in (Tinker's generator, the camp lights).

## Type and UI
- Silkscreen (8 px grid): all small UI text at 8 px, headings at 16 px, logo at 32 px.
- Pixelify Sans (16 px grid): dialogue prose at 16 px only.
- Fonts imported with antialiasing, hinting and subpixel positioning off. Both are SIL Open Font License (licences in `art/fonts/`).
- Panels: salvaged-terminal frame (`art/ui/panel.png`, 9-slice, 8 px margins). Focused option: teal bar with a cursor notch.
- Theme lives in `art/ui/theme.tres` (project theme). Rebuild with `tools/build_theme.tscn` after changing `UIKit.build_theme()`.

## Region identities
The full five-region plan (mood, light, palette, shapes, terrain, town, enemies, boss, shard) is in `docs/REGIONS.md`. Built so far:
| Region | Tileset | Backdrop | Key colours |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Heap (outdoors, open sky) | Painterly junk ground + girder and container platforms | Dusk: smog sun, hazy megacity with the dark Spire, trash conveyors, toppled crane, buried brass giant, junk hills | Burnt orange to slate teal sky, rust, oxidised green, warm rims |
| The Intake (corp machinery, indoors) | `heap_industrial` plates + painted girders | Rust-red bay: press columns, flywheel, faded "07", roof light shafts | Deep rust red, black, hot orange |

## Terrain (Room.solid_styles)
- `ground`: flat warm-brown body, sparse pressed-in debris (tyre, panel, pipe, gear, rod, bottle), a lumpy top edge lit by the sun with moss tufts and the odd rebar; dark left edges, warm right edges, shaded undersides. Edges stay within 2 px of the collision box.
- `girder`: painted rust-red I-beam, sunlit top flange, lightening holes, rivets. Vertical when tall and thin.
- `container`: oxidised-green shipping container with ridges, end frames, rust patches.
- `tiles`: the room's tileset (used indoors).
- Outdoor rooms have no ceiling; grapple hooks hang from something visible (a cable between the cliffs, or roof girders).

## Backdrops
Hand-authored point lists, no randomness. Aerial perspective: far layers lighter and warmer (melting into the haze), near layers darker. World lights never affect backdrops.

## Effects and presentation (modern layer over the pixel art)
The art stays pixel-exact; presentation is modern. Every effect is tied to a gameplay event or a real light source.
- **Lighting:** each room sets an ambient level (`Room.ambient`, a CanvasModulate). Real PointLight2Ds sit on lamps, neon, fire, docks, warning lamps, pickups, grapple anchors, the thrown blade, enemy shots, the boss core and the robot itself. Props set `light_radius`/`light_energy`; warning lamps blink their light with their frame.
- **Post-processing** (`shaders/post_fx.gdshader`, between world and HUD): bloom only on true light sources (threshold 0.93), a gentle per-region grade, light vignette, a red edge pulse on damage and a faint red edge at 1 health. No lens fringing.
- **Atmosphere:** golden dust drifting in the low sun; embers from fires, smoke from the running generator, sparks from Rusty's faulty neon, grit from the Intake chutes. Rain is available per room but off in the Heap.
- **Combat:** cyan slash arc on every swing (shows exactly where it hits), pixel spark bursts on hits, light trail and moving light on the thrown blade, glowing tether, dash afterimages, landing and dash dust, shockwave rings on the boss slam, kills, dock use, revival and the shard.
- **Camera:** smooth follow with look-ahead in the facing direction and a downward peek on long falls; sub-pixel motion (no transform snapping).
- **UI motion:** menus ease in, the dialogue box rises in, toasts fade in, the boss bar shows a lagging chunk of recent damage, lost health cells flash.
- **Toggles (Settings):** screen shake, hit-stop, post effects, flashing effects.
- Guard rails: bloom never blows out character colours (keep lights near bright brass/yellow surfaces low), ambient never so dark that the robot loses its shape, effects never cover enemies or telegraphs.

## Town residents: one family per town (`Lineage`)
Each town's residents share a common ancestor. A `Lineage` resource (`data/lineages/`) holds what everyone inherits; `NPCLook` paints each resident from it plus a seed, so looks are stable forever and no two residents are twins.
- **Inherited by every Heapfolk resident:** hunched, bundled silhouette; round head; long patchwork coat stitched with the same centre seam; oversized boots; coats cut from the same salvaged cloth (dusty brown/olive range, patched with container green, rust and canvas); and the family signature, amber-lensed goggles that glow faintly at dusk.
- **Rolled per resident:** height, build (stout ones carry a belly), hunch (with a back hump), coat shade, 1 to 3 patches, headwear (hood, cap, bandana, beanie or bare head), goggle style (single, double, pushed up), scarf colour, accessory (pack, tool belt), skin and hair.
- **Designer pins:** an NPC can pin age (adult, teen, child, elder), headwear, goggles or accessory to make a character read as who they are (Tinker: cap, goggles up, tool belt; Rusty: bandana, apron; Pip: child in a hood; Gran Oxa: elder, grey hair, cane; Sprocket: teen lookout, beanie, lantern).
- **New town = new Lineage:** give the Underworks folk their own silhouette, palette and signature (and add any new trait to `NPCLook`).
